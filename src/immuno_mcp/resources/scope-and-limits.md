# Scope and limits

immuno-mcp supports research in immunology, bioengineering and computational antibody design.

## Scope

- Research use only. Not clinical decision support.
- Not for engineering pathogens, and not for designing escape from vaccines, antibodies or other countermeasures.

## What the scores mean

- pLDDT, PAE, ipTM and ipSAE describe how confident a structure model is. They are not binding affinity (KD).
- A high interface score does not mean a high measured affinity. Check contacts, clashes and the intended epitope.
- Developability flags from sequence are predictions. They need experimental follow-up.

## Provenance

Every result lists its source, a version or retrieval date, and the URL. Scores in the workspace
ledger also record the tool, version, parameters and job that produced them.
